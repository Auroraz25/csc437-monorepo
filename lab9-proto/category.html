About this Category

Fiction books are literary works created from the imagination, not presented as fact. Fiction can be in any form, such as a novel, novella, short story, or play, and the imagination of its creators drives the story. It's often categorized by genre, such as mystery, romance, or fantasy.

Fiction works may be partly based on factual occurrences, but always contain some imaginary content.
