Writing Style Observations

F. Scott Fitzgerald's writing style is characterized by its rich, lyrical prose and its emphasis on themes of aspiration and disillusionment. He masterfully uses symbolism to convey deeper meanings, particularly in The Great Gatsby where symbols like the green light and the eyes of Doctor T.J. Eckleburg take on significant importance.

His works often explore the Jazz Age and the American Dream, with a focus on wealth, class divisions, and moral emptiness beneath glamorous exteriors.

Literary achievements include being considered one of the greatest American writers of the 20th century, with The Great Gatsby recognized as his masterpiece.
