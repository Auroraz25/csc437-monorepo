Review

Date: April 8, 2025

The Great Gatsby is a masterpiece that captures the essence of the Jazz Age in America. Fitzgerald's beautiful prose and symbolic depth create a powerful portrait of the American Dream's corruption and the hollowness of wealth. The character of Jay Gatsby remains one of literature's most fascinating creations.

Favorite quote: "So we beat on, boats against the current, borne back ceaselessly into the past."

Rating: 5/5 stars
